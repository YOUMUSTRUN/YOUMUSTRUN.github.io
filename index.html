What the file says
  1. A red marker on the floor means a hand rises there in about a second. Shoot it and the thing falls back.
  2. Arms reach from the walls, outlined in red. High ones: slide. Low ones: jump.
  3. Hands sometimes drop monster flesh. Keep it. Eating it brings you back once.
  4. Pits drop you from above, at the cost of one injury.
  5. Q raises the shield for 8 seconds. It blocks one injury, once per run. It never recharges.
  6. Hot strips on the floor mark a pit. Jump before the last one.
  7. Every injury brings it closer. The third one and it eats you.
  8. Land the needle in the red arc to push it back. Miss and it laughs.
  9. The gun hits what is in your lane. Use it on what you cannot dodge.
